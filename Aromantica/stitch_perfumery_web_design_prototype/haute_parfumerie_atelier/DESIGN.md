## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the rare, bespoke intimacy of a Parisian haute parfumerie atelier. It addresses an audience of discerning connoisseurs, olfactive collectors, and luxury tastemakers who seek artisanal provenance over mass prestige. 

The aesthetic is **Luxury Editorial Minimalist with Tactile Glass Accents**. It balances high-fashion publishing aesthetics with surgical e-commerce clarity. The interface must evoke:
- **Intimacy & Reserve:** Restrained spatial compositions, generous negative space, and unhurried pacing.
- **Sensory Provenance:** Rich contrast evoking amber resins, cold cut crystal flacons, and gold leaf stamping.
- **Architectural Precision:** Razor-thin hairline frames, micro-caps labels, and strict geometric proportions paired against expressive classical serif titling.

Avoid generic commercial cues: no loud sale badges, aggressive cart animations, or harsh shadows. Every interaction should feel as measured as extracting rare botanical absolutes with a glass dropper.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in an obsidian nocturne environment with warm alabaster duality, illuminated by brushed champagne gold and aged brass foil accents.

### Palette Architecture
- **Obsidian Dark Surfaces:** Deepest canvas `#0F0F10`, primary surface layer `#171719`, elevated container `#222226`.
- **Alabaster Light Alternative (Editorial Modals/Inverted Panels):** Pristine canvas `#FBF9F5`, secondary paper surface `#F5F2EB`.
- **Metallic Metallurgical Accents:**
  - **Champagne Gold (`#C5A880`):** Primary interactive focus, active states, and focal iconography.
  - **Muted Brass (`#B89758`):** Deep metallic accents, hover boundaries, and secondary badge outlines.
  - **Bright Foil Gold (`#D4AF37`):** Strict micro-highlights, certified extract badges, and olfactive tier callouts.
- **Hairline Borders:**
  - Dark Mode: `#2A2A2E` (subtle structural divider) and `rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.2)` (gold foil emphasis).
  - Light Mode: `#E8E2D8` (warm alabaster divider).
- **Text & Content:**
  - Primary: `#FBF9F5` (Alabaster White, 95% opacity).
  - Secondary: `#A5A29B` (Muted Pumice).
  - Subtle / Tertiary: `#6E6B65` (Smoked Umber).

### Usage Rules
Reserve `#D4AF37` strictly for rare accents: gold foil borders, active accord tags, and formula seals. Never use pure `#FFFFFF` or raw `#000000`; always preserve warm, papery undertones.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy relies on a high-contrast union between literary romanticism and technical modernism:

- **Editorial Serif (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for fragrance designations, olfactive chapter titles, maison narratives, and quote pullouts. Set primarily in regular and medium weights with slight italic inflections for notes (e.g., *Head, Heart, Base*).
- **Geometric Sans (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Grounds functional e-commerce interactions—concentrations (Extrait de Parfum vs. Eau de Parfum), milliliter capacities (50ml / 1.7 fl. oz.), formula batch IDs, and pricing comparison tables.
- **Micro-Caps Protocol:** Metadata headers, tracking notes, and accordion triggers must use `label-caps` in uppercase with wide letter spacing (`0.15em`) to evoke luxury packaging debossing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on an editorial grid calibrated for contemplative, spacious presentation:

- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Fixed canvas max-width of `1440px`, flanked by `margin: 4rem` and unified with `gutter: 1.5rem`. Wide editorial showcases can span full-bleed or utilize 8-column asymmetry alongside 4-column formula data panels.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Fluid gutters at `1.25rem` with margins at `2rem`. Two-column comparison blocks collapse gracefully into side-by-side swipeable canvases.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Unified gutters at `gutter-mobile: 1rem` and margins at `margin-mobile: 1.25rem`. Complex comparative matrices (e.g., sillage, longevity, raw ingredient source) shift to sticky-header comparison ribbons.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Generous section spacing (`space-xl` doubled for major chapter divisions) provides room for imagery of flacons, raw botanicals, and formulation journals without cognitive clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial layering evokes smoked glass vitrines, flacon translucency, and architectural gallery plinths rather than simulated physical drops:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - *Base Void:* `#0F0F10`
  - *Atelier Surface (Cards/Shelves):* `#171719`
  - *Overlay Layer (Drawers, Modals):* `#1C1C1F`
- **Foil Boundaries & Low-Contrast Outlines:**
  - Default containment uses a 1px hairline border of `#2A2A2E`.
  - Elevated or hover items adopt a subtle champagne gold stroke: `1px solid rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.35)`.
- **Glassmorphism (Vitrines & Fixed Navbars):**
  - Background: `rgba(23, 23, 25, 0.72)`.
  - Backdrop Filter: `blur(20px) saturate(140%)`.
  - Rim Highlight: Top border `1px solid rgba(245, 242, 235, 0.08)`, bottom border `1px solid rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)`.
- **Luminous Ambient Glow (No Dropshadows):**
  - Avoid black dropshadows. Use a localized gold amber haze for focused elements: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The form language is strictly **Sharp (`roundedness: 0`)**.

- **Flacon-Inspired Geometry:** Clean right angles reflect crystal cut flacons, stone perfume counters, and heavyweight bespoke carton packaging.
- **Exceptions:** Olfactive accord radar pills or botanical ingredient swatch tags may use an absolute minimal internal border (`rounded: 0.125rem`), but primary frames, buttons, image vitrines, and comparative drawers must remain strictly orthogonal (0px radius).
- **Hairlines:** All separators, border boxes, and grid boundaries must measure strictly 1px in thickness.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary CTA ("Acquire Flacon", "Reserve Batch"):** Sharp rectangle, solid champagne gold background (`#C5A880`), text in `#0F0F10` (`label-caps`, uppercase, bold). On hover, subtle shift to `#D4AF37` with an ambient gold glow.
- **Secondary CTA ("Request Blotter Sample", "Explore Notes"):** Transparent fill, 1px border in `#C5A880`, text in `#FBF9F5`. Hover activates an inner fill of `rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.08)` and border brightens to `#D4AF37`.
- **Text Link:** Set in `label-caps` with an underline positioned 4px below text (`1px solid rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.4)`). Underline expands to 100% width on hover.

### Olfactive Accord Chips & Badges
- **Formulation Chips (e.g., "Grasse Rose Absolute", "Orris Butter"):** Sharp rectangular pill, height 28px, background `#171719`, 1px border `#2A2A2E`, text in `body-sm` (`#A5A29B`).
- **Active / Filtered State:** Background `rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.1)`, border `1px solid #C5A880`, text `#FBF9F5`.

### Fragrance Comparison Matrix Cards
- Surface background `#171719` bounded by `1px solid #2A2A2E`.
- Card head features product portraiture staged against soft gradient spotlighting.
- Sillage, projection, and longevity are expressed via minimal hairline bar meters: track `#2A2A2E`, fill `#C5A880`.
- Price displayed in `title-md` using `Plus Jakarta Sans`, accompanied by price-per-milliliter calculation in `body-sm` muted tone (`#6E6B65`).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Text Inputs (Search, Concierge Inquiries):** Flat transparent background with bottom-border-only styling (`1px solid #2A2A2E`). On focus, border transitions to `#C5A880` with zero outline jump. Label positioned above in `label-caps` (`#6E6B65`).
- **Volume Selector (50ml / 100ml / 250ml Flacon):** Segmented sharp toggle bar. Selected segment receives `#2A2A2E` fill with a `1px solid #C5A880` boundary.

### Olfactive Pyramid Display (Specialty Component)
- Three-tiered stacked architectural module displaying Head, Heart, and Base notes.
- Separated by razor-thin `1px solid #2A2A2E` horizontal rules.
- Column 1: `label-caps` identifier in `#C5A880`.
- Column 2: Note names set in `Playfair Display` italic (`headline-sm`), with harvest origin notes displayed in `body-sm` below.
